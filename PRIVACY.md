# Privacy Policy

**ActualPrice**  
Last Updated: January 4, 2026

## Overview

ActualPrice ("we", "our") is a SaaS platform that provides AI-powered pricing optimization recommendations for e-commerce merchants based on social sentiment analysis. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect data.

## Data We Collect

### From Our Customers (Merchants)
- Account information (email, name, business details)
- E-commerce store data (product catalogs, pricing) via authorized OAuth connections
- Pricing rules and preferences
- Usage data related to our platform

### From Public Social Media Sources
- We collect publicly available posts and comments from social media platforms (including Reddit) related to product categories and keywords
- We analyze this content for sentiment scoring
- We do **NOT** track individual user profiles or personal information from social media users

## How We Use Data

### Social Media Data (Including Reddit)
- Content is processed to extract sentiment scores (positive/negative/neutral)
- Sentiment data is used to generate pricing recommendations for merchants
- We retain social media content temporarily for analysis and audit purposes
- We do **NOT** track or identify individual social media users
- We do **NOT** use social media data for AI/ML model training beyond sentiment analysis
- We do **NOT** resell or redistribute social media data

### Merchant Data
- Used solely to provide pricing recommendations and platform features
- Shared only with authorized e-commerce platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce) to apply price updates with merchant approval
- Never sold to third parties

## Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Social media content (mentions) | 90 days |
| Sentiment analysis results | 90 days |
| Aggregate sentiment scores | 1 year |
| Merchant account data | Duration of account + 30 days |
| Pricing history | 1 year |
| Price recommendations | 1 year |

## Data Deletion

- Merchants may request full data deletion by contacting us
- We comply with deletion requests within 30 days
- When a merchant deletes their account, all associated data is removed

## Third-Party Services

We integrate with:
- **Shopify** - E-commerce platform (via authorized OAuth)
- **WooCommerce** - E-commerce platform (via API credentials)
- **Reddit API** - Public content for sentiment analysis (read-only access)
- **OpenAI** - AI-powered sentiment analysis and content generation
- **Google Gemini** - AI-powered sentiment analysis (fallback)

## Security

- All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Database access restricted and encrypted at rest
- OAuth tokens and API credentials stored securely using encryption
- Regular security audits and monitoring
- Access controls and authentication required for all data access

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Export your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent for data processing

## GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA):

- You may contact us to exercise your GDPR rights

Our service is intended for business use only. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18 years of age.
